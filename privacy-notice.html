Privacy Notice

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Beyond Paraplanning & Intelligent Adviser Solutions ("Beyond", "we", "our" or "us") is committed to protecting personal data and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Notice explains how we collect, use, store and protect personal data when providing paraplanning, technical consultancy and business support services.

2. Data Controller & Data Processor

In most circumstances Beyond acts as a Data Processor on behalf of the financial advice firm engaging our services. The adviser or advice firm remains the Data Controller and is responsible for ensuring an appropriate lawful basis exists for processing personal data.

3. Information We Process

Depending on the services provided, we may process:

  • Personal identification information.
  • Financial and investment information.
  • Pension and protection information.
  • Tax information.
  • Health or vulnerability information where relevant to financial advice.
  • Meeting notes and adviser communications.
  • Documentation supplied by the adviser to enable us to deliver our services.

We only process information necessary to perform the services requested by the Data Controller.

4. How We Use Personal Data

We use personal data solely for legitimate business purposes including:

  • Preparing suitability reports and technical documentation.
  • Undertaking financial planning research.
  • Preparing cashflow models and technical analysis.
  • Supporting advisers with consultancy services.
  • Communicating regarding ongoing work.
  • Maintaining quality assurance and compliance records.

5. Technology Providers & Sub-processors

To deliver our services securely and efficiently we may use carefully selected third-party technology providers ("sub-processors").

These may include providers of:

  • Cloud storage and document management.
  • Email and communications.
  • Workflow and project management.
  • Artificial Intelligence ("AI") tools.
  • Business productivity software.

Where a third-party provider processes personal data on our behalf, we ensure appropriate contractual, technical and organisational safeguards are in place in accordance with UK GDPR.

An up-to-date list of our principal sub-processors is available upon request.

We never sell personal data or permit third parties to use it for their own marketing purposes.

6. Use of Artificial Intelligence

Beyond may use Artificial Intelligence ("AI") technologies to assist with administrative and technical tasks such as:

  • Document drafting.
  • Technical research.
  • Document analysis.
  • Information summarisation.
  • Workflow automation.

AI is used solely as an assistive tool.

No suitability report, recommendation, technical opinion or client-facing deliverable is issued without appropriate human review, professional judgement and quality assurance.

7. International Transfers

Some technology providers may process personal data outside the United Kingdom. Where this occurs, appropriate safeguards are implemented in accordance with UK GDPR, including recognised international transfer mechanisms where required.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to provide our services, satisfy legal or regulatory obligations and protect our legitimate business interests. Information is securely deleted or anonymised when no longer required.

9. Security

We employ appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against accidental loss, unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or destruction. These measures include encrypted storage where appropriate, controlled access to systems and secure technology providers.

10. Your Rights

Where Beyond acts as a Data Processor, requests to exercise rights under UK GDPR (including access, rectification, erasure or restriction) should normally be directed to the relevant financial adviser or advice firm acting as the Data Controller. We will assist the Data Controller where required.

11. Changes to this Privacy Notice

We may update this Privacy Notice from time to time to reflect changes to our services, technology providers or legal obligations. The latest version will always be published on our website.
